Progression

Each narrow skill requires two new problems in different contexts, with both a numerical answer and a selected explanation. Only first-attempt responses without hints qualify. Looking at a lesson, running a simulation, or clicking through never opens the next skill.

This is a provisional readiness rule, not proof of durable mastery. Immediate, structurally similar problems are limited evidence of transfer. Later project performance and delayed checks still need to be built. A later difficulty flags the specific skill without relocking completed work.

Scope & standards

Space means position and displacement; time means elapsed time. This is a designed introductory route, not a required TEKS sequence. It assumes signed numbers, basic arithmetic, units, and simple algebra. It does not teach astronomy or relativistic spacetime.
