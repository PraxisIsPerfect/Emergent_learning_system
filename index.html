EMERGENCE / PROTOTYPE NOTES

A small universe. A real learning loop.

What works here

Two selectable concepts in a real three-dimensional coordinate system, projected into a canvas. Drag to orbit, pinch or use + / − to zoom. The galaxy is a map of ideas, not an astronomical model.

The free-fall simulation is deterministic. Checks are authored questions, not an AI tutor. Each check records its context, response, hint use, and time. A missed or assisted check queues only that specific claim for another check.

What evidence means

A correct response is evidence from one short task, not mastery of “Gravity” or “Time.” Revisiting the same question after feedback is marked as assisted. A new, unassisted check in another context can clear its review flag, but does not certify transfer.

Time passing alone never marks a skill as forgotten. The “next relevant opportunity” queue is a transparent prototype rule, not a validated spaced-repetition algorithm.

Data & boundaries

Your evidence stays in this browser’s local storage. No account, analytics, API calls, microphone, camera, or cloud learner profile. Export or reset it in Evidence. A different browser or device has a separate record.

These are internal concept IDs, not official TEKS identifiers. Standards alignment is pending. The physics slice is Newtonian free fall and elapsed-time measurement; gravitational time dilation is outside its scope.
