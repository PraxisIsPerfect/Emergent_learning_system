Organization is not progression

A concept has one home and one evidence record. Prerequisites connect capabilities across planets and systems. A system glows when at least one of its implemented capabilities is available. It does not imply every activity inside is ready.

Measurement is housed in Mechanics and shared across systems. Oscillation needs elapsed time. Light-signal timing needs speed. The first spacetime activity needs displacement and elapsed time—not all of Mechanics or Quantum Physics.

Current scope

Six systems and 28 planets are mapped. Thirteen capabilities have interactive exploration and generated applications: ten retained from the previous version, plus period/frequency, light travel time, and event coordinates. Planets with dashed outlines have no activities yet; they are not locked behind fabricated prerequisites.

The spacetime activity uses one spatial dimension and one frame. Time dilation, Lorentz transformations, curved spacetime, orbital mechanics, complete circuit instruction, thermodynamics, and quantum instruction are not implemented.

Evidence

Each capability requires two new first-attempt numerical responses with correct explanations in two authored context families, without hints. This is an introductory readiness policy, not a validated mastery or transfer threshold. Watching or manipulating a model gives no credit. Later difficulty flags a capability without relocking downstream work.

Missions reuse the same capability records. These are short guided task sequences, not yet open-ended physical projects. Answers and recent attempt records are local to this browser. The finite question generators can eventually repeat; recognized repeats are practice only.

Standards & sources

This organization and prerequisite graph are design decisions, not a mandated Texas sequence. Existing activities address introductory slices of PHYS.5.C, 5.E, 5.F, and 5.H. Period and frequency connect to 8.C. Completing these tasks does not establish full standards attainment.

Relativity bridges are extensions: Einstein Online: events and spacetime. Other references: TEKS Guide: period and frequency; NASA: ideal free fall.

Storage & access

No AI, analytics, account, microphone, camera, or learner server. Progress from v2 is imported once when available on this same browser origin. Changing the hosting domain does not transfer local storage. Export or reset in Learning record. The latest 500 detailed attempts are retained; the seen-question index is kept to prevent repeated questions receiving credit.

All controls have keyboard equivalents and reduced-motion support. Map brightness is paired with outline and lock cues. Static SVG remains visible if scripts or canvas fail.
